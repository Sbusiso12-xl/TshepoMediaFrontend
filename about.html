Tshepo Media Network

About Us

A community media network built on verified information, real conversations and measurable impact.

Our Story

Tshepo — hope — started as a microphone and a promise: that credible information should reach the people who need it most. Today Tshepo Media Network is a community broadcast platform, publishing verified opportunities and hosting the conversations that shape local futures.

Our Mission

To connect communities with verified employment, education, business and development opportunities, and to amplify the voices that mainstream media too often overlooks.

Mission Statement

Tshepo Media Network is committed to giving hope a voice by providing communities with reliable and verified information about employment, education, entrepreneurship and development opportunities. Through podcasts, digital media, community programmes and strategic partnerships, we aim to connect people with businesses, government institutions and organisations, promote meaningful conversations and empower communities across South Africa.

What we stand for

Truth first

We verify before we publish. Every listing is checked against its source.

Voice

Community members are not an audience — they are contributors.

Impact

We measure success in placements, enrolments and businesses started.
