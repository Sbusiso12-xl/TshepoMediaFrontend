Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Tshepo Media Network ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly, in accordance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we use and protect it.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you use our website:

  • Contact details you provide, such as your name, email address and phone number, when you submit our contact form or subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Messages or enquiries you send us through the contact page or WhatsApp.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device type and pages visited, collected automatically to help us improve the site.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send you our newsletter with verified employment, education, business and development opportunities.
  • Respond to enquiries submitted through our contact form or WhatsApp.
  • Understand how visitors use our site so we can improve content and functionality.
  • Communicate with partners and community members about programmes, workshops and events.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Newsletter Communications

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we will use your email address solely to send you opportunity listings and podcast updates. You may unsubscribe at any time using the link in any newsletter email, or by contacting us directly.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website uses the following third-party services, which may process limited technical data as part of their normal operation:

  • Google Fonts, for website typography.
  • YouTube, to embed our podcast videos and promotional content.
  • WhatsApp, to allow you to message us directly.

These services are governed by their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the personal information we hold from loss, misuse, unauthorised access or disclosure.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law.

7. Your Rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal information.
  • Object to how your information is processed, including opting out of marketing communications.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa if you believe your information has been mishandled.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please contact us:

  • Email: hello@tshepomedianetwork.co.za
  • WhatsApp: Chat with us
  • Contact form: contact.html
